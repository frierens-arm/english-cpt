# Zero Day - Full Presentation Script
## ENL1W CPT - Book Marketing Presentation

---

### SLIDE 1 - Title
*[5 seconds]*

Nothing to say. Let them see the title. Nod. Move on.

---

### SLIDE 2 - The Hook
*[20 seconds]*

What if your entire town was built on a lie?

*[pause]*

What if the air you breathed was slowly killing you and nobody in power cared?

*[pause]*

That is Drake, West Virginia. The setting of Zero Day. A town that has been drained of its life by greed and industry. Where the truth is buried just as deep as the coal mines.

---

### SLIDE 3 - Book Summary
*[30 seconds]*

A family is murdered in Drake, West Virginia. A coal-mining town that is basically rotting from the inside out.

John Puller is an Army CID agent. That is the Army own detective unit. He gets sent to investigate. He teams up with Sam Cole, a local detective who has lived in Drake her whole life.

They keep digging. They find out the mining company has been poisoning the water and the air for years. People have been getting sick. People have died. And anyone who tried to speak up kept disappearing.

The conspiracy goes all the way up. Government officials. Company executives. Cover-ups that go back decades. And now someone is killing to keep it all buried.

It is basically a story about greed, silence, and one soldier who refuses to look away.

---

### SLIDE 4 - The Investigation
*[20 seconds]*

Step one: A family is murdered. Brutal crime scene. Step two: Puller arrives from the Army. Step three: He meets Sam Cole, the local detective who has her own reasons for helping. Step four: They start pulling the thread and the whole conspiracy starts coming apart.

CID stands for Criminal Investigation Division. Basically the Army own detectives.

---

### SLIDE 5 - What They Find
*[20 seconds]*

It was not just one crime. It was an entire system built on silence.

The company, Coaling Energy, dumped toxic waste into the water for years. They knew what they were doing.

The cover-up: Government officials took payouts. Reports got buried. Whoever spoke up got silenced. Or killed.

The cost: People got cancer. Kids got sick. Graves kept filling up. Nobody in charge said a word.

---

### SLIDE 6 - Setting: Drake, West Virginia
*[20 seconds]*

Drake is not just a backdrop. It is basically its own character.

Cookie-cutter houses. Rusted trucks. Dead grass. Coal dust on every surface. Sulfur dioxide in the air. Baldacci describes a town that is broke and sick. The mines are still running but the people are not getting anything from it.

The setting is the most important thing to show because it makes the mystery feel real.

---

### SLIDE 7 - Theme 1: Environmental Destruction
*[25 seconds]*

Drake is a town that is died. Coal mining destroyed the land. this is shown in the form of dead grass, Toxic water, Sulfur dioxide in the air and Coal dust everywhere. the author describes a town that is broke and sick. The mines continuously run but the residence of Drake is not getting anything in return

This book is not based of any real life scenarios but most of the things that happen, also happen in real life. Whenever we all use tissues, we pull it out from a tissue box, use it, then throw it out. Companies treat a place like tissues.

That is basically what happened in Flint, Michigan. The water was poisoned with lead for years. Government officials knew and did nothing. Citizens including kids, seniors and many other people got sick.

---

### SLIDE 8 - Theme 2: Corruption and Power
*[25 seconds]*

The people in charge did not overlook what was happening. They chose to ignore it because stopping it cost money.

Anyone who tried to expose the truth got killed. That is how serious it was. Company executives and the local government are picking profit over people.

Look at the Volkswagen emissions scandal. The company cheated pollution tests for years. They knew their cars were pumping out toxic fumes but they lied anyway because fixing it cost too much. Same pattern as Drake but instead of cars it is coal

---

### SLIDE 9 - Theme 3: Trauma and Trust
*[25 seconds]*

Puller is a soldier trined in combat but Drake is a place he is not trained for. The enemy is not on a battlefield but it is people in suits, a system and a corporation.

The hardest part for Puller is trust. Who can he believe in a town where everyone has been silenced?

Sam Cole left Drake and came back because she lost people to the same sickness killing the town. For her this is personal reason

This is not just in the book. People who try expose corrupt corporations or governments lose everything.

---

### SLIDE 10 - Key Characters
*[20 seconds]*

John Puller is disciplined, relentless, and loyal. He does not stop. Even when the conspiracy goes higher than he thought. He fights for people who cannot fight for themselves.

Sam Cole grew up in Drake. She left. She came back. She lost people to the same sickness killing the town. For her this case is about more than justice. It is about home.

They are an unlikely pair. Together they are dangerous to the people who want the truth buried.

---

### SLIDE 11 - Memorable Moments and Lessons
*[25 seconds]*

The crime scene. Puller arrives at the murdered family house. The details are brutal. It is clear this was not random. It was a message. Small crimes often hide much bigger problems.

Howard Reed. Seeing how coal mining has physically broken people like him shows the victims are not just the ones who were murdered. It is the whole town. Industrial greed has a permanent human cost.

When Puller realizes the conspiracy goes all the way up. A local murder becomes a massive threat. You cannot always judge a situation by how it looks on the surface.

Puller and Sam learning to trust each other. Two people from different worlds figuring out how to work together while everything falls apart. People can overcome their differences for a common goal. Like finding the truth.

---

### SLIDE 12 - English Skills
*[20 seconds]*

This book is also useful for learning real English skills.

Setting as character: Drake is not just where the story happens. It is basically its own character. The town is dying and that shapes everything.

Types of conflict: Man vs. Society when Puller fights a corrupt system. Man vs. Self when he figures out who to trust in a town built on secrets.

Voice and tone: Puller talks in a military way that is different from how a regular person would explain things. Baldacci uses voice to make each character sound different.

Teachers could use this book for class discussions about environmental justice, or for analyzing atmosphere in writing. It works with other course texts about outsider perspectives and corrupt systems.

---

### SLIDE 13 - Why High Schoolers Should Read This Book
*[30 seconds]*

This book matters because the problems in it are real.

Coaling Energy poisoned Drake's water and air for years. That is basically what happened in Flint, Michigan. Government officials knew the water had lead in it. They did nothing. Kids got sick. People died. Same pattern as Drake. Just a different town.

The book also shows how companies abuse their power. Coaling Energy knew they were dumping toxins and covered it up because fixing it cost too much. That happened in real life with Volkswagen. They cheated pollution tests for years while their cars pumped out toxic fumes. Same thing. Different industry.

This book is about stuff that is actually happening. Environmental damage. Corporate greed. People in charge choosing profit over lives. If you want a thriller that makes you think about the real world, this is it.

---

### SLIDE 14 - Marketing Campaign
*[25 seconds]*

If I was marketing this book to new readers, here is what I would do.

The book cover uses dark mountains and thick fog. Dark colors to signal danger. That is a thriller convention. The soldier walking toward a tunnel uses isolation as a visual. The tagline at the bottom sets up the main conflict. Truth versus secrets.

The movie poster concept takes it further. Fog covers the town. That is a visual trick for mystery and hidden danger. The lone figure on an empty road shows the outsider entering a hostile space. Dark colors and simple layout create tension before you read a single word.

The point is: the visuals do the work. They tell you what kind of story this is before you read a single line.

Some secrets are worth killing for.

That is what this book is about. Thank you.
